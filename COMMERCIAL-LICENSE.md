## What AGPL-3.0 means for you

The AGPL is the strongest copyleft license: its obligations trigger not only when you
distribute the software, but also when you make it available to users **over a network** (SaaS
/ hosted use). In short, if you modify Kosmos and let others use it — including as a hosted
service — you must make your complete corresponding source code (including your modifications)
available to those users under the AGPL.

If you're self-hosting Kosmos for your own business and are willing to comply with the AGPL,
**you do not need a commercial license.** It's free.

## Who needs a commercial license

You likely need a commercial license if you want to:

- **Modify Kosmos privately** and not release your changes.
- **Embed Kosmos** in a proprietary product you distribute.
- **Offer Kosmos as a service** (hosted / SaaS) without releasing your modifications under the
  AGPL.
- Operate under a corporate policy that **prohibits AGPL** software.

A commercial license removes the AGPL's copyleft obligations for your use, under terms agreed
in writing.
