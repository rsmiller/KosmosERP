# Kosmos ERP — Services

Kosmos ERP is open source (AGPL-3.0). The software is free; getting it running well in
*your* environment — your data, your workflows, your identity provider — is where I help.

I'm the person who builds Kosmos, so you're working directly with the implementer, not a
reseller or a support queue. Below is the full offer ladder, from a free fit call to ongoing
support. Every engagement is fixed-scope and fixed-price so you know the number up front.

## The offer ladder

### 1. Discovery / Fit Assessment — free
A 30-minute call to see whether Kosmos is a fit for your operation. Honest answers only — if
it's not a fit, I'll tell you.

- **Format:** 30-minute video call.
- **You get:** a one-page "does Kosmos fit you" note summarizing fit, risks, and next steps.
- **Cost:** $0.

### 2. Paid Pilot — $2,500–5,000 (fixed)
A time-boxed proof, in *your* environment with *your* real data, for one workflow — for
example, sales order → production → shipment.

- **Timeline:** 2–3 weeks.
- **You get:** Kosmos stood up for one real workflow you can evaluate hands-on.
- **Why:** de-risks the larger investment before you commit to it.

### 3. Full Implementation — $10,000–25,000+ (fixed scope)
The anchor engagement. End-to-end setup so Kosmos runs your business.

- **Includes:** data migration, configuration, provider wiring (your Stripe / Keycloak /
  storage), training, and go-live.
- **You get:** a working, owned ERP — a higher-touch, dramatically lower-cost alternative to
  six-figure incumbent quotes.

### 4. Support Retainer — $1,500–2,500/mo
The goal of every implementation: someone who knows your system on call.

- **Includes:** guaranteed response SLA, updates, small changes, and a monthly check-in.
- **Terms:** auto-renews monthly.
- See [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) for how paid support compares to free community support.

### 5. Enterprise Auth / Custom Module Add-ons — priced per project
Premium capabilities built on request: SAML/OIDC SSO, SCIM provisioning, advanced RBAC, and
custom modules specific to your operation. Scoped and quoted per project. If you need
something Kosmos doesn't do yet, see [SPONSOR-A-FEATURE.md](SPONSOR-A-FEATURE.md).

### 6. Commercial License — for buyers who can't use AGPL
If AGPL-3.0 doesn't work for your intended use (private modification, embedding in a
proprietary product, or offering Kosmos as a service without releasing changes), a commercial
license is available. See [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md).

## How engagements progress

The natural path is **land-and-expand**: fit call → paid pilot → full implementation →
support retainer → add-ons. I don't sell a full implementation cold — we start with a fit
call and, usually, a pilot, so both sides know it will work before the big spend.

Every paid engagement begins with a short written proposal: problem → scope → deliverables →
fixed price → timeline → terms. Nothing starts until the scope and number are agreed in
writing.
