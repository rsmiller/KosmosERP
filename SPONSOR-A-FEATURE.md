# Sponsor a Feature

Need something Kosmos doesn't do yet? You can sponsor its development.

- Open an issue describing the outcome you need.
- I'll scope it and quote a fixed price + timeline.
- On payment, it's built, tested, documented, and (unless it's an enterprise-tier feature)
  merged into the open-source release so everyone benefits.
- Sponsors are credited in the release notes (opt-out available).

---

## How it works

1. **Open a feature request.** Describe the outcome you need — the problem, not just the
   implementation — in a [GitHub issue](../../issues). The public roadmap lives in
   [GitHub Projects](../../projects).
2. **I scope and quote it.** You get a fixed price and timeline before any commitment.
   Sponsorable issues are labeled **`sponsorable`** with a rough estimate.
3. **You sponsor it.** On payment, the work jumps the queue and gets built.
4. **It ships.** Built, tested, and documented — merged into the open-source release so the
   whole community benefits, unless it's an enterprise-grade feature that belongs in a paid
   tier.
5. **You're credited** in the release notes (opt-out available).

## Pricing

Most sponsored features land in the **$2,000–8,000** range, depending on scope. Larger or
enterprise-grade work (e.g., SSO/SCIM, custom modules) is scoped as a project — see
[SERVICES.md](SERVICES.md).

## Payment

- Invoice from the business (preferred for companies), or
- A one-time GitHub Sponsors tier, or
- Polar.sh.

Contact [More Guids](https://www.linkedin.com/in/more-guids/) to arrange payment, or just open the issue and we'll sort it out
there.

## Why this exists

Sponsoring a feature de-risks the roadmap — work gets built because someone needs it — and
every sponsored feature makes Kosmos more capable for the next user. You get exactly what you
need, on a firm timeline, from the person who built the system.
