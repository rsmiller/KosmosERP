# Kosmos ERP — Support

There are two ways to get help with Kosmos: **free community support** and **paid support with
a guaranteed SLA**. Use whichever fits your situation.

> **Need guaranteed response times?** [More Guids](https://www.linkedin.com/in/more-guids/)

---

## Community support — free

Best-effort help from the project, in the open.

- **Bug reports & feature requests:** open a [GitHub issue](../../issues).
- **Questions & discussion:** use [GitHub Discussions](../../discussions) (or issues if
  Discussions isn't enabled).
- **Response:** best-effort, no guaranteed timeline. Community-visible so others benefit.
- **Cost:** free.

Community support is the right choice if you're evaluating Kosmos, self-hosting for a hobby or
internal project, or comfortable running it yourself. Please search existing issues before
opening a new one, and include your version, steps to reproduce, and relevant logs.

> Free support does not include private/priority responses, phone or video help, guaranteed
> turnaround, custom changes, or production incident response. Those are covered by a paid
> retainer below.

---

## Paid support — retainer with SLA

For businesses running Kosmos in production, a **Support Retainer** gives you a direct line to
the person who builds it.

- **Price:** $1,500–2,500/mo (based on scope and response tier).
- **Includes:**
  - Guaranteed response SLA on issues you raise.
  - Product updates and upgrade assistance.
  - Small changes and configuration adjustments each month.
  - A monthly check-in to keep your system healthy and aligned with your roadmap.
- **Terms:** month-to-month, auto-renews.

A retainer is the natural continuation of a full implementation — see the offer ladder in
[SERVICES.md](SERVICES.md).
