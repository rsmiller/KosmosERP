What's inside

The core of a manufacturing ERP, in plain modules that map to how a shop already works — from the quote through to the shipment.

SO

Sales Orders

Quote to order to invoice. Follow every job from the customer PO through to what actually ships.

MFG

Production

Work orders, routings, and a production schedule your floor can read and follow — not a whiteboard.

INV

Inventory

Live stock counts, lot and serial tracking, and reorder points that finally replace the spreadsheet.

PUR

Purchasing

Purchase orders, supplier records, and receiving tied back to the jobs the parts were bought for.

SHIP

Shipping & Fulfillment

Pick, pack, and ship with the paperwork generated for you instead of retyped every time.

CRM

Customers & Leads

Leads, contacts, and activity history so nothing slips between the first call and the closed job.

QA

Quality & Traceability

Inspections, nonconformance, and full lot traceability for audits, certifications, and recalls.

RPT

Reporting

Dashboards and clean exports for the numbers owners and controllers actually ask for.

IAMEnterprise

Identity & Access

SAML/OIDC single sign-on, SCIM user provisioning, and real role-based permissions — enterprise auth done right.

Every module ships in the open-source core. Identity & Access is offered as an enterprise add-on. Not sure a module fits your process? That's what the fit call is for.
